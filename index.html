Who we are

MDEI is an industrial engineering firm focused on delivering high-integrity automation, robotics integration and cybersecurity services for production environments. Our multidisciplinary team blends control engineering, systems integration and OT security practices to deliver dependable, maintainable systems at scale.

PLC / SCADA architectures, electrical panels, instrumentation and telemetry for process control and energy efficiency.

Robotics — Integration

OT risk assessments, network segmentation, intrusion detection and incident response tailored to production networks.

Turnkey automation projects: End-to-end delivery from engineering and panels to commissioning and HMI/SCADA deployment.
